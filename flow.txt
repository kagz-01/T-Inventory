Yes — **Touchline Inventory (TI)** can be much more than a stock counter. It can become the internal **operations control center** for Touchline.

The mockup I generated is built around that idea:

### TI — Touchline Inventory

**Core purpose:**

> **Track. Manage. Execute. Grow.**

It should let the boss see what is happening across the business, while employees use it to record what they receive, use, source, complete, and deliver.

### The really useful part

Every important action should create an **activity trail**.

For example:

> **08:42 — James received 20 sheets of 3mm clear acrylic**
> Supplier: SignWorld
> PO: TL-0845
> Quantity: +20
> Location: Main Store

Then:

> **10:15 — Mary issued 4 acrylic sheets to Job #TL-1028**
> Project: ABC Corporate Reception
> Quantity: -4
> Approved by: David

Then:

> **14:30 — James completed "Cut acrylic reception letters"**
> Job: #TL-1028
> Materials used: 4 sheets acrylic
> Status: Completed

That gives the boss a **traceable history of what happened**, rather than relying on verbal updates or notebooks.

### Employee side

An employee shouldn't see everything the administrator sees.

For example:

**Employee dashboard**

```text
Good morning, James 👋
```

### Boss/Admin side

The boss gets the bigger picture:

```text
TODAY AT TOUCHLINE

Inventory       1,248 items
Low Stock          17
Pending Orders     18
Active Jobs        12
Employees          12
Tasks Completed    36

          ↓

WHERE IS MONEY GOING?
Material purchases
Supplier costs
Production costs
Job profitability

          ↓

WHAT IS EVERYONE DOING?
Employee activity
Task completion
Attendance
Job progress
```

That distinction is important: **TI shouldn't just be "inventory software."**

It becomes:

> **Touchline's internal operating system.**

And because you're building it for Touchline itself, I'd design it so the system eventually connects **inventory → supplier → employee → task → production → customer order → completed job → cost/profit**.

That would make TI a genuinely useful internal platform rather than just another stock-management dashboard.
